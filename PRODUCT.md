# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: a dependency-free Node.js API gateway with a Python processing worker, plus vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so the prototype runs offline without package installation.

## Users

The business workflow serves supermarket and kirana owners and hotel and restaurant procurement teams. The intelligence workflow serves food-safety analysts, compliance teams and researchers. Faculty reviewers assess the final academic demonstration.

## Product Purpose

FoodSafe Intelligence turns official cached FSSAI/FIRA rejection records into searchable, linked evidence. Businesses can compare a stock item with historical alerts, read the match reasons and decide which supplier documents to request. Analysts can inspect incidents, entities and computed patterns.

## Positioning

The product provides historical evidence and purchasing support through transparent product matching and connected regulatory records. A check cannot certify a current shipment, supplier or batch.

## Operating Context

The prototype is demonstrated live on a laptop and must remain functional without internet access. It should support a five-minute guided story and also allow free exploration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Core capabilities include a product evidence check, searchable sample catalogue, local sample-barcode lookup, regulatory search and filters, incident details, entity profiles, relationships and analytics. There are 18 official cached FIRA incidents, 27 normalized entities, and a separate catalogue of 36 illustrative products in 12 categories. Product checks compare product terms, categories and origins using Python rules. Supplier, batch and barcode data do not contribute a regulatory-match score. The final demo runs locally without accounts or cloud services.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

- Evidence before inference.
- Explain every match and relationship.
- One dataset powers every view.
- Demo reliability over production complexity.
- Honest prototype labels over unsupported claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web interface should support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, and narrow-screen layouts.
